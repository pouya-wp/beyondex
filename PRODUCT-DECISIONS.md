# Current product and pricing decisions

- Each agent has its own independent management UI. Beyondex manages subscriptions, renewals and panel access; it does not host the agents' work interface.
- There is no free subscription. Each agent offers two limited trial runs; enforcement and activation await the external subscription service.
- Toman price levels: Essential 10,000,000/month, Professional 35,000,000/month, Premium 120,000,000/month; Enterprise quoted separately.
- Annual monthly equivalents: 10,000,000, 30,000,000 and 100,000,000 Toman respectively. Annual checkout totals are 12 times these rates.
- Price levels guide discovery. Pricing CTAs lead to agent selection; each agent's page displays its own final catalog price (10–120 million Toman/month).
- Existing paid USD rates are preserved. Previously free Essential now has a USD rate of $99/month. Currency prices are independent, not automatically exchanged.
- Unsupported free-monthly quotas, certifications, blanket security guarantees, and refund promises were removed from active pricing/FAQ/checkout content.
- Contact/newsletter previews validate locally, preserve entered content, and explicitly state that delivery/registration is not connected. No new infrastructure was added.
- Payment guards, authentication and provisioning readiness remain unchanged. Do not enable sales until the separately documented backend blockers are resolved.
